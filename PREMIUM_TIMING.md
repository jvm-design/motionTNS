# 🍎 Premium Apple Timing - Quality Over Speed

## 🚨 **The Problem: 700ms Was TOO FAST**

### **User Feedback:**
> "I got the impression that all happens too fast and users ain't gonna perceive a quality motion that guides their experiences"

### **The Issue:**
- **700ms total** = Everything rushed
- **117ms convergence** = Invisible blur
- **Can't follow the motion** = No guidance
- **Feels cheap** = Not premium
- **No appreciation** = Wasted craftsmanship

## 🍎 **The Solution: 1500ms Apple Premium Timing**

### **Philosophy:**
> **"Fast enough to feel responsive, slow enough to be SEEN and APPRECIATED"**
