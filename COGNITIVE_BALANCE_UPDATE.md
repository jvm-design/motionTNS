## 🎯 Apple Motion Principles Applied

### **1. Recognition Before Action**
> "Users need time to understand what they're looking at before movement begins"

**Before:** Logo flashed for only 50ms
- ❌ Too fast to process
- ❌ Disorienting
- ❌ Feels rushed

**After:** Logo visible for 167ms
- ✅ Sufficient recognition time
- ✅ User understands context
- ✅ Prepared for action

---

### **2. Balanced Cognitive Weight**
> "Start and end states should have equal importance"

**Before:**
- Logo: 50ms (7% of animation)
- Checkmark: 200ms (29% of animation)
- **Ratio: 1:4** (heavily skewed)

**After:**
- Logo: 167ms (24% of animation)
- Checkmark: 183ms (26% of animation)
- **Ratio: 1:1.1** (balanced) ✅

---

### **3. Fast Responsive Actions**
> "Actions should feel immediate and confident"

**Before:** Convergence took 250ms
- ⚠️ Felt sluggish during shutter sound

**After:** Convergence takes 133ms
- ✅ Snappy and responsive
- ✅ Completes before shutter ends
- ✅ Maintains camera sync at 300ms
